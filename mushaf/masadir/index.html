نص خانة App Review Information (بالإنجليزية)
Hello App Review Team, "Mushaf al-Shawahid" (مصحف الشواهد) is a free educational app for students and teachers of the Quran. It shows the Mushaf in the Warsh reading and links each word to the verses of the classical didactic poem "Mawrid al-Zam'an" on Quranic orthography (rasm) and diacritical marking (dabt), with the classical commentaries on it. Content and rights: - The classical texts were written by scholars who died centuries ago: al-Kharraz (d. 1318), al-Tanasi (d. 1494), Ibn Ajatta (14th century), Ibn Ashir (d. 1631) and al-Marghini (d. 1930). These works are in the public domain. Only the authors' own text is used, without the notes of modern editors. - The Quran text (Warsh, version 10), the Mushaf page images (Madinah Mushaf, Warsh) and the KFGQPC Warsh font come from the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex, which provides them free of charge. They are used unmodified and are not sold. - All other fonts (Amiri, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Reem Kufi, Omniversify Maghrib) are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the license texts are included in the app. - A "Sources and Rights" page inside the app lists every book, author and license. Privacy: the app is free, has no ads, no in-app purchases, no account or login, and collects no personal data. Settings and saved items stay on the device only. No login is required to review the app; all content is available from the first launch, including offline. The app was compiled and prepared by Khaled Kadoum, a Quran teacher at the Model Quranic School, Emir Abdelkader Mosque, Constantine, Algeria. Thank you.
